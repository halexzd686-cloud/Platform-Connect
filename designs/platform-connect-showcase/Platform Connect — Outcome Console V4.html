AI 不会直接抢走你的工作，但会先拿走其中一部分

我们常把“工作”想成一个完整岗位，于是问题总是：AI 会不会取代我？ 但更值得观察的是，你每天做的事情正在被重新拆分：重复、可描述、能快速验证的任务，更适合交给 AI；目标设定、价值判断和结果责任仍然需要人。 真正要练习的，不只是写提示词，而是识别：哪些判断不能外包。 收藏这份判断框架，也欢迎说说你工作中最不能交给 AI 的部分。

FINAL THREAD ENGLISH

AI may not replace your job. It will unbundle it first.

1/ We keep asking whether AI will replace entire jobs. That framing hides the change already underway. 2/ Jobs are bundles of tasks. The repetitive, describable and quickly verifiable parts are the first to move. 3/ What stays human longer: setting goals, judging trade-offs and carrying responsibility for outcomes. 4/ The useful AI skill is not prompting alone. It is knowing which judgments you should never outsource. 5/ Before automating your role, map the decisions that make the role yours.
