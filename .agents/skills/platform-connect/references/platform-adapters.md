# Platform adapters

These are editorial defaults, not a substitute for checking current publishing specifications. Do not state volatile limits or policy details unless verified at task time.

## Douyin

- Lead with a conflict, misconception, result, or immediate question.
- Use short spoken sentences and fast progression.
- Deliver one clear payoff; move supporting detail into caption or follow-up content.
- Output: title options, opening hooks, spoken script, caption, cover phrase, CTA, suggested duration.

## Xiaohongshu

- Frame the content as an experience, decision aid, checklist, comparison, or reusable method.
- Make the structure easy to scan and save.
- Avoid exaggerated personal testimony when the source contains none.
- Output: title options, video script or note body, section labels, caption, topics, cover phrase, CTA.

## WeChat Channels

- Favor trust, complete reasoning, and a shareable point of view.
- Keep the relationship between speaker, audience, and claim clear.
- Output: spoken script, title options, publishing note, share text, cover phrase, CTA.

## Bilibili

- Establish the question and viewing promise, then organize the explanation into chapters.
- Preserve nuance and supporting logic; do not reduce a complex thesis to a slogan.
- Output: title options, opening, chaptered script, description, cover phrase, dynamic post, CTA.

## Kuaishou

- Use direct, concrete language and recognizable situations.
- Let examples carry the explanation; avoid unnecessary abstraction.
- Output: hooks, spoken script, caption, cover phrase, CTA, suggested duration.

## TikTok

- Build around one tension and one fast reward.
- Localize idiom, reference points, and CTA for the selected language and market.
- Output: hook variants, concise script, caption, cover phrase, CTA, suggested duration.

## YouTube Shorts

- Make one specific promise and fulfill it within the short script.
- Use a compact setup, explanation, and takeaway.
- Output: title options, hook, script, description, cover phrase, CTA, suggested duration.

## YouTube long-form

- State the viewing promise, define the question, and use chapters to develop the argument.
- Preserve evidence, counterpoints, and useful nuance.
- Output: title options, opening, chapter outline, full script, description, thumbnail phrase, CTA.

## Instagram Reels

- Open with a relatable scene, visual contrast, or concise insight.
- Favor clean, quotable phrasing without turning every claim into inspiration language.
- Output: hook, concise script, caption, cover phrase, CTA, suggested duration.

## Facebook Reels

- Start from a recognizable everyday situation or conversation-worthy question.
- Make the payoff understandable with sound off and allow the caption to carry context and links.
- Localize social references and CTA for the selected market; avoid assuming the audience knows Chinese-platform conventions.
- Output: title options, hook, short script, caption, cover phrase, CTA, suggested duration.

## LinkedIn

- Lead with the practical professional implication, not an unsupported personal success story.
- Structure the post or video around an observation, evidence-grounded explanation, and a discussion question.
- Preserve qualifications, trade-offs, and B2B or career context from the source.
- Output: post opener, structured body or video script, carousel outline when appropriate, discussion prompt, CTA.

## X

- Reduce the source to one defensible point of view, then add only the evidence needed to support it.
- Use a single post for a concise insight or a numbered thread for reasoning that needs sequence.
- Make the first line stand alone; avoid clickbait that overstates the source.
- Output: single-post version, optional thread, media caption, CTA or discussion prompt.

## Threads

- Write in a conversational, reflective voice that invites replies rather than performs a broadcast.
- Let the opening feel personal only when the source supports that viewpoint; otherwise state the observation directly.
- Use short paragraphs and one open-ended closing question.
- Output: post variants, optional short thread, visual caption, reply prompt.

## Pinterest

- Turn the article into a saveable utility: a checklist, guide, comparison, process, or inspiration board.
- Make the title and description search-friendly without promising results unsupported by the source.
- Plan vertical visual assets with a clear first-frame value proposition.
- Output: pin title options, pin description, on-image headline, asset concept, CTA.

## Snapchat Spotlight

- Use an immediate visual situation and an informal spoken explanation.
- Keep one simple takeaway per video and let the first seconds establish the context without a long preamble.
- Localize references, humor, and CTA for the selected market.
- Output: hook options, spoken script, on-screen beat list, caption, CTA, suggested duration.

## Cross-platform QA

- Each version should sound native to its destination, not merely differ in length.
- Hooks, script, cover text, and CTA must promise the same outcome.
- Every version must remain traceable to the shared content brief.
- Flag unsupported medical, legal, financial, safety, or performance claims for review.
